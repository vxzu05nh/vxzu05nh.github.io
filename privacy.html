PDB Reader 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-27 · 适用于 PDB Reader（app.pdb.reader）

PDB Reader 不收集任何个人信息。没有账号，没有统计，没有广告。你导入的文档和你的阅读数据只保存在你自己的设备上。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

没有。应用不要求注册或登录，不包含统计分析、广告、崩溃上报之类的第三方 SDK，也不向我们的或任何第三方的服务器发送数据——我们没有任何服务器接收这些数据。

2. 你的数据存在哪里

  • 导入文档时，应用会把文件复制到自己的私有目录；此后阅读不依赖原文件的位置。
  • 书架记录、书签、标注、上次阅读位置与各项设置保存同一处。
  • 卸载应用或清除应用数据会删除以上全部内容。我们没有副本，也无法为你恢复。

3. 网络访问

应用在正常使用中不发起网络请求。如果文档内容里含有指向网站的链接，点击后由你的系统浏览器打开——之后的访问受那个网站自身的隐私政策约束，与本应用无关。

4. 文件访问权限

应用只访问你主动选择导入、或通过系统的「打开方式 / 分享」交给它的文件。它不会扫描或读取你设备上的其他文件。

5. 儿童

本应用不面向儿童设计，也不收集任何人的数据。

6. 测试版本（TestFlight）

如果你通过 TestFlight 安装测试版，Apple 会依其 TestFlight 条款收集崩溃日志与使用数据。这部分由 Apple 控制：我们只能看到 Apple 提供的崩溃报告与汇总统计，无法从我们的代码里获得你的任何信息。

7. 本政策的变更

本政策如有变更，会更新本页顶部的日期。我们不保留历史版本，因为不涉及任何已收集的数据。

8. 联系方式

隐私相关的问题或请求，请联系：vxzu05nh@outlook.com

PDB Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27 · Applies to PDB Reader (app.pdb.reader)

PDB Reader collects no personal information. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. The documents you import and the data you create while reading stay on your own device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app requires no sign-up or sign-in, bundles no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and sends no data to our servers or anyone else's — we do not operate a server to receive it.

2. Where your data lives

  • Importing a document copies it into the app's private container, so reading later does not depend on the original file's location.
  • The shelf, bookmarks, annotations, last reading position and settings are stored in the same place.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of it. We hold no copy and cannot restore it for you.

3. Network access

The app makes no network requests in normal use. If a document contains a link to a website, tapping it opens your system browser; what happens after that is governed by that site's own privacy policy, not by this app.

4. File access

The app only reads files you pick yourself, or that the system hands it through Open With / Share. It does not scan or read other files on your device.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

6. Beta builds (TestFlight)

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage data under its TestFlight terms. That collection is Apple's: we only see the crash reports and aggregate statistics Apple provides, and nothing in our code gives us access to your information.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. No version history is kept, because there is no collected data to account for.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: vxzu05nh@outlook.com
